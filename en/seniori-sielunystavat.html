Seniors' Friendship Club

Joy to the soul and happiness to the heart. We invite everyone who feels lonely or lacks warm and heartfelt communication. Friends make good times better and bad times easier.

Hello! We are Janetta and Aidas, the facilitators

In a cozy and safe environment, you can express your feelings and experiences, receive support, share your dreams, and gain the determination to realize them.

The duration of the meeting is 2 hours. Each session also includes 5–10 minute breaks, during which we do light physical exercises and learn simple self-defense skills, as well as the basics of karate.

Who is this group for?

Join us and find a warm-hearted community where everyone feels valued. To join, you need to attend a preliminary interview where we will get to know each other and answer your questions.
