Activities Promoting the Wellbeing of Multilingual Older People

The aim is to reduce loneliness and prevent social exclusion among older people who speak languages other than Finnish, while strengthening social relationships, peer support and a sense of participation.

MUMILI Friendship Group

The MUMILI Ystäväkerho group provides a safe and confidential environment where participants can meet others, share their experiences and build new social connections in their own native language. For many, the group becomes their first stable social network in Espoo.

Who is it for?

The activities are intended for Espoo residents aged 65 and over who speak languages other than Finnish, including immigrants and Ukrainian refugees. The activities are free of charge and open to everyone in the target group.
